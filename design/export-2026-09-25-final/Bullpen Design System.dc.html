Bullpen · paper-trading league

A trading floor at night, not a casino.

Tokens and components for the landing page and the trading app. Every number shows how fresh it is, every motion explains a change, and nothing relies on color alone.
01
Rooted in the Tiarê Balbi system
Saira for display, Nunito Sans for UI, JetBrains Mono for every price, quantity and P/L.
02
Ember is the one accent
Same in both themes. Light mode uses a deeper ember ink for text so it passes AA.
03
Direction is never color alone
Mint and red, always with ▲/▼ and +/−. The color-blind palette swaps to blue and orange.
04
Ghost hairlines are allowed
A deliberate break from the No-Line Rule: dense data needs edges. Tonal layering still comes first.
05
Springs that don’t bounce
Layout springs are critically damped, so they settle without overshoot, as the brand’s motion rules require.
06
Names map to React
TickerBadge, PriceCell, Sparkline, OrderTicket, OrderTimeline, LeaderboardRow, DiagramNode…
